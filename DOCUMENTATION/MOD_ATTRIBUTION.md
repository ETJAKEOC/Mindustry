# Mod Attribution & Credits

This fork of Mindustry incorporates content, features, planets, campaign maps, units, blocks, turrets, items, liquids, and code directly from the amazing community mods listed below.

We express our sincere gratitude to all original authors and contributors for their creativity, hard work, and dedication to the Mindustry ecosystem.

---

## Integrated Mods

### 1. Vanilla Expansion (VE)
- **Authors**: 火星人076 & 128_OTEML
- **Repository**: [Martian238/Vanilla-Expansion-Mod](https://github.com/Martian238/Vanilla-Expansion-Mod)
- **Description**: Introduces the planet Maress, Serpulo post-PLT campaign extension, new sectors, blocks, units, and custom campaign logic.

### 2. Extra Utilities
- **Author**: guiY
- **Repository**: [guiYMOU/mindustry-Extra-Utilities-mod](https://github.com/guiYMOU/mindustry-Extra-Utilities-mod)
- **Description**: Utility blocks, specialized logic components, and quality-of-life additions.

### 3. New Horizon Mod
- **Author / Team**: New Horizon Team (Yuria Shikibe)
- **Repository**: [Yuria-Shikibe/NewHorizonMod](https://github.com/Yuria-Shikibe/NewHorizonMod)
- **Description**: Large content expansion featuring custom teams, advanced turrets, heavy units, unique mechanics, and custom maps.

### 4. Asthosus
- **Author**: Catser
- **Repository**: [Catana791/Asthosus](https://github.com/Catana791/Asthosus)
- **Description**: A deserted planet, Asthosus, with 32 playable campaign & optional maps, custom music, over 180 unique pieces of content (blocks, liquids, items, units, status effects).

### 5. Exoprosopa
- **Author**: TnmSpiroct
- **Repository**: [TnmSpiroct/Exoprosopa](https://github.com/TnmSpiroct/Exoprosopa)
- **Description**: Frostnova planet expansion featuring 26 campaign maps, 21 items, 188 blocks, 20 units, and custom environment hazards.

### 6. Advance Content
- **Author**: EoD (Advance Creations Corporation)
- **Repository**: [EyeOfDarkness/AdvanceContent](https://github.com/EyeOfDarkness/AdvanceContent)
- **Description**: Adds 40+ turrets, 10+ units, items, liquids, and industry blocks.

### 7. Extended Unit Arsenal
- **Author**: Garmy
- **Repository**: [Garmundo/Extended-unit-arsenal](https://github.com/Garmundo/Extended-unit-arsenal)
- **Description**: Vanilla-styled unit expansion featuring fighters, capital ships, new turrets, and challenging campaign sectors.

### 8. ExoGenesis
- **Author**: AureusStratus
- **Repository**: [AureusStratus/ExoGenesis](https://github.com/AureusStratus/ExoGenesis)
- **Description**: Extensive content mod with custom items, liquids, weapons, and unit tiers.

### 9. Mindustry Calamity
- **Author**: -Undaria-
- **Repository**: [Undaria/MindustryCalamity](https://github.com/Undaria/MindustryCalamity)
- **Description**: Mod inspired by Terraria Calamity featuring over 300 pieces of content, blitzkrieg campaign mechanics, and boss units.

### 10. Schematics Pack
- **Author**: pixecoz (Special thanks to Qwaka)
- **Repository**: [pixecoz/SchematicsPack](https://github.com/pixecoz/SchematicsPack)
- **Description**: Collection of pre-built schematics and schematic demonstration maps.

### 11. Time Control
- **Author**: sk7725
- **Repository**: [sk7725/TimeControl](https://github.com/sk7725/TimeControl)
- **Description**: In-game speed slider allowing acceleration and deceleration of game time.

### 12. MindustryX
- **Author / Team**: TinyLake / MindustryX
- **Repository**: [TinyLake/MindustryX](https://github.com/TinyLake/MindustryX)
- **Description**: Community content enhancements and assets.

### 13. Revid AI Enhancer
- **Author**: VortexLarkWorship
- **Repository**: [VortexLarkWorship/revid-ai-enhancer](https://github.com/VortexLarkWorship/revid-ai-enhancer)
- **Description**: AI enhancer reference and workflow tools.

### 14. Kotlin Runtime
- **Author / Organization**: xpdustry
- **Repository**: [xpdustry/kotlin-runtime](https://github.com/xpdustry/kotlin-runtime)
- **Description**: Integrated Kotlin standard library (`kotlin-stdlib`), `kotlin-reflect`, and `kotlinx-coroutines` runtime environment to enable native execution for Kotlin-based Mindustry mods without requiring an external runtime mod download.
